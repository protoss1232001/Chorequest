⭐ ChoreQuest Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The short version: ChoreQuest collects nothing.

What data the app handles

ChoreQuest stores the family data you enter — children's first names or nicknames, avatars, chores, points, rewards, and PINs — only on the device the app runs on, in the browser's or app's local storage.

What leaves the device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests after it loads. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no cookies, and no third-party services. We (the developers) never see, receive, or store any of your family's information.

Children's privacy

Because no personal information is ever collected or transmitted, ChoreQuest does not collect personal information from children as defined by COPPA (US), GDPR-K (EU), or similar regulations. A parent sets up all profiles; children need no account and provide no information to anyone.

Backups

The optional backup feature creates a file on your device at your request. Where you store or send that file is entirely under your control.

Data deletion

Deleting the app (or clearing the site's browser data) permanently erases everything. There is nothing to delete anywhere else, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Changes and contact

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
